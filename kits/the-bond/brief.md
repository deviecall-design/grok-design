# The Bond

Pet wellness. Warm, quiet, and physical. A note about a specific animal, not a marketplace and not a clinic dashboard.

## Palette

Sage and clay on a cream that is warmer than Eka Noe's sand. Dark mode is a brown room, lighter and softer than Eka Noe's terminal black, and the accent stays green. Headings stay the serif. The two kits do not share a hue.

## Separate from Eka Noe

Eka Noe is the investing terminal (`kits/eka-noe`). The Bond does not share its folder, tokens, type, or voice. Do not put a price, a P&L, or a "paper trading" label on a Bond screen. Do not put sage, clay, or the serif display onto Eka Noe.

## Voice and material

Headings use the foundation serif (`font.family.display`). Body stays the sans. Sage (`color.accent`) is the action colour. Clay (`color.clay`) is a secondary material for a small mark or a short label, with `color.on-accent` if the clay is a fill. It is not a second brand system and not a rainbow of pet colours.

Copy is specific and calm. Name the animal, the care, the date. No baby-talk, no paw emoji, no pastel gradient.

## Do not

- Do not borrow Echo Oncology's clinical chrome, research-use pattern, or illustrative-data surface. If a Bond screen must separate a recorded fact from a general tip, say **Recorded** and **General** in text and ask before adding tokens.
- Do not use emoji as icons.
- Do not centre a care log. A short introductory page may be quiet and sparse. It is still left-aligned.
