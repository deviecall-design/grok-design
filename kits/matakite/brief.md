# Matakite

Rugby analytics for performance staff. Dense enough to compare, quiet enough to read for a long session.

## Branding boundary

Do not use NZRU, the All Blacks, the silver fern, or any union, franchise, or team name, nickname, crest, or mark as branding or decoration. Do not imply an official partnership. Do not style the product as a black jersey with silver.

Refer to sides the way the dataset does, or as home and away. Do not invent national-team styling. Chart series are `color.series.a` (clay) and `color.series.b` (deep teal). If a third series is required, ask. Do not add black, white, or silver as a series to "complete the strip".

The clay accent is the product's. It is not a claim on a club colour.

## UI

Sans for headings and body. `font.family.mono` for player identifiers, times, and scores. Tables are left-aligned with `color.line` rules. One primary action. No sports-poster hero, no crowd photography as chrome, no emoji balls.

## Do not

- Do not letter a screen with a team motto or a haka reference as decoration.
- Do not reuse the Mirofish or Eka Noe terminal themes. This is a light analytics surface.
