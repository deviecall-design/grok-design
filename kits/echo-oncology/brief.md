# Echo Oncology

Clinical, calm, and honest. Information first. The screen should feel precise, not like a spa and not like an alarm.

## Palette

Near-neutral cool grey, with one slate-teal accent. Light is a clinical paper. Dark is a blue-black field, still calm. Illustrative content keeps a warm ground in both modes so it cannot sit on the measured surface. Chart series are teal, ink, clay, and slate. `color.info` stays a quiet steel.

## Labels

Any screen that can be read as medical advice carries **Research use only**, in `color.status.research`, where the reader is looking. A footer line is not enough.

Measured data and illustrative data never share a colour.

- Measured values use `color.data.measured` on `color.surface` or `color.bg`, with the word **Measured** when the two kinds could be confused.
- Illustrative values use `color.data.illustrative` on `color.data.illustrative-surface`, with the word **Illustrative** on that surface.

Do not chart an illustration in the measured colour. Do not put a measured number on the warm illustrative surface.

`color.danger` is for a risk the product has defined, and the risk is still named in words. It is not a theme colour. Do not decorate with crosses, hearts, or emoji.

## Tone

Cool paper, white measured surfaces, one slate-teal accent used rarely. Sans for both headings and body. Air is allowed. Decoration is not. Left-align records, tables, and forms.

## Do not

- Do not imply a diagnosis, a treatment effect, or a patient outcome the data does not state.
- Do not reuse Mirofish, Eka Noe, or The Bond tokens to make a chart "friendlier".
