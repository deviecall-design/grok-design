# Eka Noe

Dark, dense, terminal-style UI for inspecting paper and simulated markets. Warm black, brass used rarely, monospace figures.

## Palette

Warm ink and brass. The brass chroma stays low so it does not glow. Dark is the working terminal. Light is a dry sand field with dark brass text, still a terminal: no sage, no serif, not The Bond's cream. Chart series are brass, clay, olive, and stone. Clay here is a chart mark, not The Bond's material colour.

## Not The Bond

The Bond is the pet wellness venture. It has its own folder and its own tokens (`kits/the-bond`). Eka Noe never shares a screen, a colour, a type stack, or a component with The Bond. Do not "soften" Eka Noe toward The Bond, and do not put market figures on a Bond screen.

## Not Mirofish

Mirofish is the other terminal kit: cool green-black and olive. Eka Noe is warm black and brass. Do not swap their accents or treat them as themes of one product.

## Labels

v1 treats figures as paper or simulated unless the task explicitly says a figure is live. There is no live-money colour. If a task needs live capital, stop and ask.

Every paper or simulated figure carries the word **Paper** or **Simulated**, in `color.status.paper`, on the same surface as the figure.

Gain and loss use `color.success` and `color.danger` plus a word or an explicit sign. Do not paint a whole row. Brass is the accent, not a warning and not a profit state. Caution text uses `color.warning`, which is intentionally hotter than brass.

## Density

Same posture as Mirofish. Small space steps inside tables, `color.line` between rows, `font.family.mono` for figures, `size.touch` for anything pressed. Sans chrome, not the foundation serif.

## Do not

- Do not use glow, gold gradients, or a candle-chart costume.
- Do not centre the workspace.
- Do not import The Bond's serif, sage, or clay.
